# Real Estate Data Analyst Primer

**DOM (Days on Market)** — The number of days a property has been actively listed for sale, counted from the listing date until it goes under contract or is sold. Lower DOM generally signals a hotter market or a well-priced property.

**Comps (Comparables)** — Recently sold properties similar in size, location, and condition to a subject property, used to estimate a fair market value.

**List-to-Close Ratio** — The final sale (close) price divided by the original list price, expressed as a percentage. A ratio above 100% means the home sold for more than asking price; below 100% means it sold for less.

**Escrow** — A neutral third-party holding period between an accepted offer and the final close of a sale, during which inspections, appraisals, and financing are finalized.

**Cap Rate (Capitalization Rate)** — For investment properties, the ratio of a property's net operating income to its purchase price, used to estimate potential return on investment.

**Price Per Square Foot (PPSF)** — A property's price divided by its livable square footage, commonly used to compare properties of different sizes within the same market.

**Pending** — A listing status meaning an offer has been accepted and the sale is in progress but not yet closed.

**HOA (Homeowners Association) Fee** — A recurring fee paid by property owners in certain communities to cover shared amenities and maintenance.
